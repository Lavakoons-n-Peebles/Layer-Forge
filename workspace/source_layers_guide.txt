=== 3D Model Print Guide ===
Layer Height: 0.08 mm
----------------------------------------
Z: 0.00 - 0.32 mm (Layers 1-4) -> Base / Foundation
Z: 0.32 - 0.48 mm (Layers 5-6) -> #000000
Z: 0.48 - 0.64 mm (Layers 7-8) -> #FFFFFF
Z: 0.64 - 0.80 mm (Layers 9-10) -> #FF0000
Z: 0.80 - 0.96 mm (Layers 11-12) -> #00FF00
